## The Problem

Airline delays cascade across the US air network. Predicting which airports will be affected, and when, is a graph problem with both spatial (route topology) and temporal (time-of-day, weather) structure. A delay at a single hub on a Monday morning can ripple through dozens of downstream airports over the next 12 hours — and traditional time-series models miss the topology that drives the propagation.

## The Approach

Modeled the US airport network as a directed graph (nodes = airports, edges = flight routes). Progressively built models from dense networks and LSTMs, through Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) and Graph Attention Networks (GAT), culminating in a Spatio-Temporal GNN combining GNN layers with LSTM to capture both structural and temporal dependencies.

The Graph Attention layer computes the attention coefficient between airport $i$ and neighbor $j$ as:

$$\alpha_{ij} = \frac{\exp(\text{LeakyReLU}(\vec{a}^T [\mathbf{W}\vec{h}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{W}\vec{h}_j]))}{\sum_{k \in \mathcal{N}_i} \exp(\text{LeakyReLU}(\vec{a}^T [\mathbf{W}\vec{h}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{W}\vec{h}_k]))}$$

This learned weighting lets the model focus on the most predictive upstream airports rather than treating all neighbors equally — critical when a single hub disproportionately drives delay propagation.

## The Pipeline

Processed Kaggle's 2018–2022 US flight delay dataset (~70M records) through a modular pipeline: preprocessing, multi-horizon training, model evaluation. Reproducible setup with YAML hyperparameter management and unit tests.

- **Preprocessing**: temporal alignment, missing-value imputation, feature scaling, train/val/test splits by date.
- **Graph construction**: directed routes, edge features (historical traffic), node features (rolling weather + delay statistics).
- **Training**: multi-horizon (1h, 3h, 6h, 12h) regression on per-airport mean delay, with masked loss for low-traffic airports.
- **Evaluation**: MAE/RMSE per horizon, plus a "cascade fidelity" metric measuring topological agreement with ground-truth delay spread.
